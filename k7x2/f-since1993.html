Story

According to the company's official history, Taimado opened in August 1993 in Amerikamura, Osaka, as Japan's first shop specialising in cannabis products.

The website went online in August 1996, and the Shimokitazawa shop opened that September.

In 1998 we opened Restaurant Asa in Shimokitazawa, serving dishes made with hemp seed.
